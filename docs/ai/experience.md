## thoughts
- Prices for AI tokens are currently skyrocketing in some cases. This is likely to continue in the near future, as hardly any provider is currently offering their models at a price that covers their costs. Chinese providers, in particular, are currently offering tokens at a ridiculously low price (presumably made possible by huge subsidies). This won’t go on forever. This is perhaps why the [economize doc](theory/economize.md) may become even more important in the future. However, I certainly do not believe that the use of AI will decline in the future.
- local excecution – it is possible to run AI models locally. At the moment, however, I wouldn’t consider this a viable alternative to the AI tools currently available. You usually have to stick to small models, and intensive use requires good hardware – one option I’ve tried myself is Ollama - local models can be used with tools such as Claude Code, OpenCode or the vs code extension Continue. Some models work surprisingly well for simple tasks (in my example, qwen3-coder). However, when it comes to complex, large-scale problems, they are usually not a viable option. It must be said, though, that many of the free AI code agents still have significant shortcomings in terms of their range of applications, usability and reliability.
