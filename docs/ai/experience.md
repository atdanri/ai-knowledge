## thoughts
- Prices for AI tokens are currently skyrocketing. This is likely to continue in the near future, as hardly any provider is currently offering their models at a price that covers their costs. This is perhaps why the [economize doc](theory/economize.md) may become even more important in the future. However, I certainly do not believe that the use of AI will decline in the future.

## other

You usually have to stick to small models, and intensive use requires good hardware – one option I’ve tried myself is Ollama - local models can be used with tools such as OpenCode, for example
